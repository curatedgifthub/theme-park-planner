# assets/ — binary and brand files

Deliberately close to empty. Going text-only cut the 38 images and 12 audio loops the project
once planned, which is what collapsed the copyright surface down to trademark and accuracy.

What legitimately lands here: the wordmark, a favicon, and any webfont — none of which can be
drawn until **Q1** settles what this is called. See the IP guardrails in the plan first; the
wordmark is the one place where Disney fonts, the castle, and the ear silhouette are *not* safe.

`assets/_source/` is gitignored: editable originals stay on your disk, and only export-ready
files get committed.
